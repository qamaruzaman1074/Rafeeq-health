For autosomal recessive conditions, when both partners carry a pathogenic or likely pathogenic variant in the same gene, each pregnancy has about a 1 in 4 chance of an affected child, a 1 in 2 chance of a carrier child, and a 1 in 4 chance of a child who inherits neither familial variant. Each pregnancy is independent.
