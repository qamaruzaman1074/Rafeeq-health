### home.counselling.heading

Technology can explain. People can guide.

### home.counselling.body

Genetic information can raise personal and complex questions. A qualified genetic counsellor can help explain results, discuss inheritance patterns, explore family history and consider available options.

RAFEEQ encourages appropriate professional guidance rather than replacing it.
