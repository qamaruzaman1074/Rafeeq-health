### howItWorks.step3.heading

Step 3 — Explore couple-level considerations

### howItWorks.step3.body

Some inherited conditions depend on the genetic contributions of both biological parents.

For example, when both biological parents carry relevant disease-associated variants in the same autosomal recessive condition, there may be a chance of an affected child in each pregnancy.

The actual risk must be interpreted using the specific condition, variants and clinical evidence.

RAFEEQ helps explain this concept without treating every condition or couple as the same.

### howItWorks.distinction.heading

Important distinction

### howItWorks.distinction.body

A carrier result is not automatically a diagnosis. A potential inherited risk is not a prediction that a child will definitely have a condition.

Clinical interpretation should be provided by qualified professionals.
