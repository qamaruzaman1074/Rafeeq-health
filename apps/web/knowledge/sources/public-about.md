### about.purpose.heading

Why RAFEEQ exists

### about.purpose.body

Genetic screening can help identify inherited health risks, but a test result alone does not guarantee understanding.

Individuals and couples may receive complex information without knowing what carrier status means, how inheritance works or what questions to ask next.

These challenges can be more significant in communities where consanguineous marriage is practised and where conversations about inherited conditions may involve family, cultural and personal considerations.

RAFEEQ addresses this gap by connecting genetic education, understandable screening information, privacy-conscious sharing and counselling navigation in one accessible platform.

### about.storyHeading

Why RAFEEQ exists

### about.story

Genetic screening can reveal important information about inherited conditions. However, the meaning of an individual finding and its potential implications for a couple may be difficult to understand without clear communication and appropriate professional support.

RAFEEQ brings education, consent-based information sharing, understandable risk explanations, and pathways to genetic counselling into one experience.

Our approach respects personal values, family relationships, cultural context, and individual privacy.
