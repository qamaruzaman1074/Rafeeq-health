### counsellingPage.heroTitle

You do not have to interpret complex genetic information alone.

### counsellingPage.heroIntro

Genetic counselling helps individuals and couples understand genetic information, inheritance patterns, possible implications, and available options. It can also help people consider uncertainty and personal values without pressure.

### counsellingPage.imageAlt

Two healthcare professionals speaking with a couple in a clinic consultation room. Stock photograph.

### counsellingPage.whenHeading

When counselling may help

### counsellingPage.when

A carrier finding is identified.

Both partners have potentially relevant findings.

Results are inconclusive.

A finding has significant potential implications.

Family history raises questions.

A person needs help understanding screening limitations.

Someone wants to explore available reproductive or clinical options.

### counsellingPage.whatHeading

What counselling can involve

### counsellingPage.what

Reviewing relevant screening information.

Explaining inheritance patterns.

Discussing possible risks and uncertainty.

Reviewing whether confirmatory assessment is appropriate.

Explaining available options.

Supporting informed, values-sensitive decisions.
