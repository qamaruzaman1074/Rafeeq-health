### privacyPolicy.title

Privacy Policy

### privacyPolicy.heroTitle

Your information deserves respect.

### privacyPolicy.intro

RAFEEQ recognises that genetic and family health information is personal. We aim to handle information responsibly, explain its intended use and support meaningful consent.

### privacyPolicy.sections.collect.heading

1. Information we may process

### privacyPolicy.sections.collect.body

Depending on the features used, information may include basic account or profile information; information voluntarily entered by a user; genetic screening information provided by a user; information related to consent and sharing preferences; and technical information necessary to operate the service.

In the demo, language, theme and reading-mode preferences may be stored in your browser. Demo sessions use temporary in-browser state and fictional example profiles.

### privacyPolicy.sections.use.heading

2. How information is used

### privacyPolicy.sections.use.body

Information may be used to provide requested platform features, explain educational content, support relevant workflows and improve service functionality.

RAFEEQ must not use sensitive genetic information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis and required consent.

### privacyPolicy.sections.consent.heading

3. Consent and sharing

### privacyPolicy.sections.consent.body

Genetic information may reveal details relevant to biological relatives as well as the person tested. Sharing should therefore be carefully considered. Users should understand what information is being shared, with whom and for what purpose before using a sharing feature.

In the couple privacy screens, separate sharing scopes are modelled (e.g. overlap with partner, sharing with clinic). Revocation is immediate in the demo UI and logged in the audit timeline.

### privacyPolicy.sections.access.heading

4. Access and permissions

### privacyPolicy.sections.access.body

Access depends on user roles, permissions and the features in use. In the demo, couple, counsellor, analyst and admin areas are separated by role. Being a partner, family member or healthcare professional does not by itself give someone access to another person's results.

### privacyPolicy.sections.minimise.heading

5. Data minimisation

### privacyPolicy.sections.minimise.body

RAFEEQ aims to collect and process only information needed to provide the requested functionality.

### privacyPolicy.sections.storage.heading

6. Storage and security

### privacyPolicy.sections.storage.body

Information security depends on the systems and safeguards in use. This policy is not a guarantee of encryption, secure hosting or regulatory certification. The demo runs in your browser with example data.

### privacyPolicy.sections.retention.heading

7. Retention and deletion

### privacyPolicy.sections.retention.body

Retention periods and deletion procedures will be defined for the live service in line with applicable legal requirements. In the demo, session data stays in your browser and can be reset at any time.

### privacyPolicy.sections.thirdParty.heading

8. Third-party services

### privacyPolicy.sections.thirdParty.body

Fonts are self-hosted. Any external service that processes user information will be identified in this policy.

### privacyPolicy.sections.rights.heading

9. Your choices and rights

### privacyPolicy.sections.rights.body

Users will be informed of the available rights and procedures for access, correction, consent withdrawal and deletion, subject to applicable law. Questions can be sent to help@rafeeq.health.

### privacyPolicy.sections.contact.heading

10. Contact

### privacyPolicy.sections.contact.body

A verified privacy contact and organisation identity must be added before publication.
